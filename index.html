About (English)

Quantum ile Piyasa is a daily Turkish-language market commentary show covering macro, Borsa Istanbul, US stocks, commodities and crypto, produced from public market data. Long episodes air every weekday morning and evening, with a crypto special on weekends; short clips are published on YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Sosyal Kralice is our in-house tool that prepares these videos and, after the channel owner approves each post, publishes them to the channel's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. How it works. This is AI-generated analysis, not investment advice.
